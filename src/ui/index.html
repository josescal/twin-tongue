How to use Twin Tongue

The panel lets you decide which audio is translated during a call and verify what the tool understood when you speak.

Version -

Before you start

  1. Check that the panel says Connected.
  2. Select your language and the other person's language.
  3. Choose a male or female voice for translation.
  4. Start the call and check that the application appears in both connections.

Conversation control

  • Audio you hear controls whether the other person reaches you translated or unchanged.
  • Audio the other person hears controls whether your voice reaches them translated or unchanged.
  • When you enable translation, wait until the panel says Ready.
  • If translation is not available, original audio remains active.

For better recognition

  • Speak clearly and keep a steady volume.
  • Use clear phrases and leave a short pause between ideas.
  • Avoid speaking over the other person when possible.
  • Reduce background noise and use headphones to avoid echo.

Review transcription

The Transcription tab shows what the tool understood and the translation sent to the other person. Use it to detect unclear words, repeat a phrase, or explain an idea differently.

Keep in mind Transcription and translation can contain errors, especially with noise, names, strong accents, or very fast speech. Translation also takes a few seconds because the system must listen, interpret, translate, and play the phrase.
